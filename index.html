Sample · Algebra

Solve 3x + 5 = 20

Question: Find x.

Show step-by-step solution

Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15.

Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.

Check: 3(5) + 5 = 20, so the answer is correct.
